Fares

One fare per passenger category, in Philippine pesos. These drive the revenue figures on the Dashboard.

Each passenger’s fare is saved when they register, so changing a fare later does not change past bookings. Leave a box empty to remove that fare. Revenue counts a booking only after staff verify it at the ticketing station.

EARLIER REGISTRATIONS WITHOUT A FARE

Passengers who registered before fares were set have no fare recorded, so they count as zero revenue. Apply the current fares to them once, after you have saved the fares above.
